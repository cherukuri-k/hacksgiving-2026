Every program we studied runs the same five steps — and skips the fourth.

02 · What we built

The brief asked five questions. One shared record answers all five.

Track 01

Information Sharing & Coordination

Organise, access and responsibly share information

Partner Network + Dispatch

Shared directory with live capacity. One tap: Jordan calls and emails the partner, records the answer, texts the volunteer a run sheet.

Track 02

Community Requests & Referrals

Manage requests, connect to services, track whether help arrived

Jordan + the Relay Board

Phone, text or web becomes one record in under a minute. Claim-to-own. Closed only when the resident confirms.

Track 03

Data Collection & Impact Measurement

Document work, own the records, measure and communicate impact

Impact Studio + Grants

Every request logs itself. Outcomes verified by residents and mapped to the City's 26 clusters. One click to a funder draft; CSV export.

Track 04

Community Awareness & Decision Support

Neighbourhood-level insight for prevention and outreach

Neighborhood Pulse

Where help is asked for and where it isn't arriving, by neighbourhood. Emerging signals and a weekly brief. Counts only — never people.

Track 05

Training & Knowledge Sharing

Prepare workers, preserve knowledge, coordinate rapid response

Volunteer Guide + Response Rooms

ComForce playbooks written once, surviving staff turnover. “Ask the Guide” answers only from those playbooks. Per-case responder chat.

One record underneath every module: a request logged by phone is the dot on the map, the row in the report and the case in the room.All five live today

05 · Responsible by design

Violence-prevention data is the most sensitive data a city holds. Relay is built so it only ever contains needs — not people.

—
Never identifies, scores or predicts a personNeeds by neighbourhood, never individuals. A breach, a subpoena or a curious volunteer finds counts — not names, risk scores or predictions. Synthetic data in every demo.
