02Jordan · AI agentAnswers and logs the requestIn English or Spanish. Asks what is wrong, and where.
03Jordan → residentTexts a tracking codeRelay MKE: your request is logged as R-4KDX.

2The work

05ComForce workerSees it on the dashboardOne card every division works from, with one owner.
06ComForce workerGets the tasks, already draftedWritten for this household. Safety steps come first.

3The proof

09ComForce workerTaps DeliveredSo far, only the helper says it is done.
10Relay → residentAsks the resident by textDid the help for request R-4KDX arrive? 1 = yes, 2 = partly, 3 = no.
11Jordan · AI agentNo reply? Jordan calls back“Yes, everything came. Thank you.”
