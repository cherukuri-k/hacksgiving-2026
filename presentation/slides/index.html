Limits what this demo does not show yet
  • Partner side is simulated in the demo — real outbound call needs a ported number
  • 2026 data is synthetic — shaped on DCWS's published cluster map, not real cases
  • Hmong is interpreter routing, not fluency — by design, until a partner validates it
  • One organisation, one board — roles per division are the next schema change
  • Verification is a text or a call — not a site visit; we report it that way
